# P1.2 Topic Bank, Playbook Loader and Exploration Share Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Execution for this slice is native.

**Goal:** Topic planning per FR-PLN-1 and FR-PLN-2.
- **The playbook becomes structured data.** A loader reads its pillars, banned list, topic bank and learned section.
- **The topic bank exists.** A starter bank across the five pillars, as a proposal Abbas confirms or edits.
- **A new read tool, `get_topic_candidates`.** It tells the orchestrator and scriptwriter the next format by share deficit, whether this post should be exploration (about 20% of a week), and topic candidates. Candidates rank never-used topics first, then the pillar used least recently, skipping any topic among the last 20 briefs.
- **`submit_brief` checks the brief's own words.** It refuses a brief whose topic, angle, hook or CTA uses a banned term or speaks in the first person, before any money is spent.

**Architecture:** `engine/plan/playbook.py` (`Playbook`, `load_playbook()`, `Topic`) and `engine/plan/topics.py` (`next_format()`, `exploration_due()`, `topic_candidates()`), both pure functions over the playbook and `briefs` rows. Tools stay in `tools.py`.

**Spec:** `docs/SRD.md` §6.5 (FR-PLN-1, 2, 3), §16, §5.4, GR-3.

## Global Constraints

- The playbook's Rules section is Abbas's: the topic bank is written as a proposal he confirms, like the other proposal sections; the engine never edits Rules.
- Exploration is a share of the identity's week (Monday start, as `get_state` counts), rounded half up: one in five at 0.2 and five posts a week.
- Topics carry no personal facts and avoid the banned list (FR-QA-6, FR-QA-8).

## Tasks

1. Loader: parse `## Rules`, `### Pillars`, `### Banned topics and claims`, `### Topic bank` (with `#### <pillar>` and `#### Explore` groups) and `## Learned`. Errors name the missing section.
2. Starter topic bank in `selfhelp.playbook.md` (proposal), about four topics a pillar plus explore topics, each passing the text checks.
3. `topics.py` and the `get_topic_candidates` tool, given to the orchestrator and scriptwriter; `get_playbook` adds the structured fields.
4. `submit_brief` runs the banned-term and claims checks on the brief's text.
5. Prompts and the dry-run director use the tool; golden tool order updated. ADR 0017.

## Review Focus

1. A topic used in the last 20 briefs must not be suggested again → `test_recent_topics_are_skipped`.
2. Exploration must stay near the share across a week, not flip every post → `test_exploration_share_over_a_week`.
3. A brief built from a banned topic must be refused before voicing → `test_submit_brief_refuses_banned_terms`.
4. A playbook missing its topic bank must say so, not return no topics silently → `test_missing_section_is_named`.
5. Every proposed topic must pass the same text checks a script must → `test_every_bank_topic_passes_the_text_checks`.

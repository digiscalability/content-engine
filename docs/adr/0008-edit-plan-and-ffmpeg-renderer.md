# ADR 0008: Edit plan semantics and the ffmpeg renderer

- Status: accepted, 2026-11-13 (slice P0.4)
- Requirements: SRD §7.1, §7.2, §7.5, §4.1, FR-PRD-5, NFR-8, NFR-9

## Context

§7.1 lists the edit plan's parts and §7.2 the renderer's duties, but leaves open the exact fields, how ops combine, how deep "ducking" goes, how loudness is reached, and how a Windows command line copes with long graphs.

## Decision

**Edit plan** (`engine/produce/editplan.py`, pydantic, unknown keys rejected):

- JSON keeps the SRD's names (`"in"`, `"out"`); shot `in`/`out` are source seconds, every other time is timeline seconds. A shot lasts `(out − in) / speed + freeze.duration`; the plan lasts the sum of its shots.
- Ops apply in a fixed order: trim → `crop` (source pixels) → `speed` → 30 fps → `reframe: cover` (scale to fill 1080×1920, centre crop) → `freeze` (hold the frame at `at`, shot-relative) → `zoom` (`from_scale` → `to_scale` over the whole shot, ease `linear | in | out | in_out`, centred). `annotate` places a HyperFrames block relative to its shot. Subject-tracking reframe is §7.3, Phase 2.
- Transitions are `cut` only. Crossfades change shot overlap maths and wait until a slice needs them.
- Shot audio is not used: format C's speech is the VO WAV (the avatar is lip-synced to it), so every sound comes from `audio`. Real-you audio with RNNoise arrives with P2.7.
- Validation refuses `out` ≤ `in`, a freeze outside the played part, annotations past their shot, overlays, VO or SFX past the plan's end, and overlapping VO segments. Format length limits (30–60 s for C) belong to the brief and are checked when a plan is submitted (P0.7).

**Renderer** (`engine/produce/render_ffmpeg.py`):

- Three ffmpeg runs: mix → loudnorm pass 1 → encode. Each reads its filter graph from a file with `-/filter_complex <file>` (ffmpeg 7+), so a long timeline never meets the 32 KB Windows command-line cap. Graph builders are pure functions with unit tests; real ffmpeg runs only under `-m golden`.
- Each shot is cut to an exact frame count (`tpad` clone, then `trim=end_frame`) so shot lengths add up to the plan to the frame (golden: 180 frames for 6 s).
- Zoom upsamples to 2× before `zoompan`, which otherwise steps visibly at small zoom rates.
- The LUT (`lut3d`, tetrahedral) grades the concatenated picture **before** overlays and captions, so graphics keep their colours. Overlays are the ProRes 4444 alpha files of ADR 0001, placed with `setpts` and `enable=between(t, start, end)`.
- **Ducking:** the SRD asks for sidechain ducking under the VO, and the plan gives a depth. A sidechain compressor alone ducks by an amount that depends on the VO's level, so the bed is split: one copy goes through `sidechaincompress` (threshold −60 dBFS, ratio 20, attack 20 ms, release 300 ms, keyed by the VO) and falls to near silence under speech; a dry copy at gain `k = 1 / (10^(duck_db/20) − 1)` is added back and the sum is scaled by `1 / (1 + k)`. Unducked, the bed is unchanged; under VO it sits exactly `duck_db` lower, and it still follows the VO's real pauses. Golden measures 11.6 dB for a 12 dB plan.
- **Loudness:** two-pass `loudnorm` to the `engine.yaml` targets (−14 LUFS, −1 dBTP) in linear mode (one constant gain), with a loose LRA target of 20 so linear mode is not abandoned. A silent mix (`-inf`) skips normalisation.
- Encode: `h264_nvenc -preset p5 -rc vbr -cq 21`, `yuv420p`, 30 fps, AAC 192 kb/s 48 kHz, `-t` the plan duration, and the shared metadata-strip flags of ADR 0001. `libx264 -crf 20` is the fallback when no NVENC is present.
- Sources arrive as resolved paths (`RenderSources`: asset ids, overlay block ids, captions, LUT). Looking assets up in the DB and looks up from config arrive with P0.7 and P0.8.

**Golden test** (`tests/golden/test_render_format_c.py`): a fixed 6 s plan over generated media checks the FR-PRD-5 probe, frame count, loudness (−14.0 LUFS measured), duck depth, freeze, LUT, overlay order and the picture around the overlay, plus six 32×56 grey frame hashes within a mean difference of 8. `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1` rewrites the hashes after someone has looked at the frames.

## Consequences

- FR-PRD-7 (`render_report.json`) and the FCPXML/OTIO exports (C7) were in no slice's text. Abbas placed them in P0.7 on 2026-11-13 (SRD D12), the first slice that renders from a real brief.
- A lesson from building the fixtures: an alpha source generated by `-f lavfi -i color=…@0` loses its alpha unless the alpha `format` is inside the same source graph. The golden test now also checks that the picture stays visible around an overlay, which caught it.
